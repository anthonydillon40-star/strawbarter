  • • Free to browse & join — no account needed for most events
  • • Click Register — you'll get all details, time, location, link

📤 Submit Your Event

  • • Free community events — always free to list
  • • Workshops / markets — basic listing free
  • • Featured / pinned — small premium fee
  • • Affiliate ticket integration — earn on sales
  • • All events moderated — safe & spam-free

💰 Revenue Streams

  • • Ticket platform affiliate fees (Eventbrite, etc.)
  • • Featured / pinned event listings
  • • Community spotlights & local business ads
  • • Premium event organizer dashboard
  • • Free events → grow traffic → all revenue streams grow!

Guidelines: All events must be legal, safe, and respectful. No commercial spamming or unauthorised ticket resales. Always check with organisers before travelling — sometimes plans change.
