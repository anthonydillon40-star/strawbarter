Find Someone To Fix It

Broken but repairable? Post what's wrong, where you are, and what you'd trade or pay. Local skilled people — electronics, woodwork, sewing, mechanics, appliances. Barter, small fee, or $STRAW. Someone nearby can fix it cheaper & better than replacing!

📖🔨💡

Learn To Fix It — Free Guides

Why pay when you can DIY? Step-by-step guides for the most common repairs: phone screen replacement, loose chair leg, broken zip, wobbly table, blender not spinning, bike puncture. Photos + video links. Start saving money today — it's easier than you think!

🧩🔩🔌

Spare Parts & Components

One broken item = a box of useful parts! Sell or swap screens, buttons, motors, cables, screws, hinges, chips, wheels, connectors. Don't bin the whole thing — someone needs exactly that one part to finish their repair. Keep components circulating — cheaper & greener!

🛠️📐🔨

Borrow & Lend Tools

Why buy a £50 drill you'll use once? Borrow from a neighbour! Power tools, specialist kits, ladders, garden machinery — all available locally. Deposit system, clear loan periods, insurance optional. Save space, save money, share what you have. Tool library for your community!

Upcycle Ideas & Showcase

Turn "trash" into treasure! Before & after photos, project ideas, material lists, tutorials. Pallet furniture, jar lights, upcycled clothing, tin can planters, denim cushions, wooden crates. Get inspired — then share your own creations! Creative, cheap, zero-waste home decor.

🛡️ Safe & Fair Guidelines

✅ Be honest: Describe the fault clearly — no surprises

✅ Agree price/swap FIRST: Parts, labour, collection — all clear upfront

✅ Meet safely: Public place, daytime — or postal with tracked delivery

✅ Set loan periods: Tools — agree return date, deposit optional

✅ Learn safely: Mains electricity = take extra care — unplug first!

✅ No dangerous items: No weapons, chemicals, recalled goods — keep everyone safe
