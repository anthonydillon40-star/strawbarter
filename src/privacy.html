🔒 Privacy Policy

1. What we collect. Account details you provide (name, email, location), listings you post, and standard technical logs.

2. How we use it. To operate the platform, verify listings, prevent fraud and provide support. We never sell your personal data.

3. Cookies & storage. We use minimal local storage (for example, to remember age verification) — no third-party advertising trackers.

4. Your rights (UK GDPR). You may request access, correction or deletion of your data at any time via the Contact page.
