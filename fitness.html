🤝 Why a Workout Buddy Works

  • ✅ 2x more likely to stick to a routine
  • ✅ Accountability = no skipping sessions!
  • ✅ Push each other further — better results faster
  • ✅ Safety — especially for early morning / evening runs
  • ✅ Split costs — gym, court hire, coaching
  • ✅ Make friends — fitness is more fun together!

💰 Ways to Save & Share

  • ✅ Buy pre-loved gear — 50–90% off RRP
  • ✅ Share memberships — split the cost, use off-peak
  • ✅ Swap passes — can't go? Pass it on for half price
  • ✅ Group buy gear — bulk discount for 3+ people
  • ✅ Swap skills — I teach yoga, you teach strength
  • ✅ Free community sessions — park runs, outdoor yoga

🛡️ Safety & Smart Tips

Meeting Buddies

  • • First meet in a public, well-lit place
  • • Tell someone where you're going
  • • Start with a casual session — no pressure
  • • Same goals = best match!

Buying Gear

  • • Check size / fit before paying
  • • Test weights, bikes, electronics
  • • Ask for receipt / proof of purchase
  • • Check for recalls — Gov.uk/check

Passes & Memberships

  • • Confirm transfer is allowed by provider
  • • Check remaining time / blackout dates
  • • Get all account details in writing
  • • Never share passwords — use official transfer
