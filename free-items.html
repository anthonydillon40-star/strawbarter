How It Works — 100% Free & Direct

🔑 No Money — Ever

  • • This section is for FREE items ONLY. No payment, no money, no fees — ever.
  • • All arrangements are direct between users. Message → agree → collect — entirely yourselves.
  • • Platform NEVER handles payments or items. We are a listing board only — nothing changes hands through us.
  • • Always inspect items in person before taking them. Meet in daylight, check condition, transport yourself.

✅ Giving Items

  • • Items must be legal, safe, and non-hazardous
  • • Be honest about condition — no surprises
  • • Arrange collection at a safe time/place
  • • Remove your ad once item is gone

🙏 Requesting Items

  • • Be polite and prompt — reply quickly
  • • Arrange collection clearly — don't no-show
  • • Bring bags/transport — come prepared
  • • Say thank you — kindness goes a long way 💚

By using this section: All items and arrangements are entirely between the giver and receiver. This platform provides listing information only — we do not inspect, verify, or guarantee items.
