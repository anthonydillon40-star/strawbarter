🛡️ Safe & Responsible Practices

  • ✅ Electrical safety first — unplug before working; if unsure, consult a qualified electrician
  • ✅ Meet in public — for material handovers & first meetings
  • ✅ Be clear about condition — describe faults honestly; set realistic expectations
  • ✅ Agree scope upfront — what's included, timeline, cost, or swap terms
  • ✅ Use proper PPE — goggles, gloves, masks for dusty/chemical work
  • ✅ Report hazards — help keep the community safe for everyone

💡 Upcycle & Repair Pro Tips

  • ✅ Assess before you start — is it worth fixing? Cost vs replacement
  • ✅ YouTube is your friend — most repairs have free step-by-step tutorials
  • ✅ One person's trash = another's treasure — always ask before discarding
  • ✅ Start small — simple repairs build confidence for bigger projects
  • ✅ Document your process — photos help others learn & build your portfolio
  • ✅ Pay it forward — teach someone what you know — knowledge multiplies!
