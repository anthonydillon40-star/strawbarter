🛡️ Safe Business Practices

  • ✅ Verify profiles — check reviews, ID, social links before high-value deals
  • ✅ Meet in public — for equipment handover, key exchange, first meetings
  • ✅ Clear agreements — scope, deadlines, deliverables, payment terms in writing
  • ✅ Start small — test with a small project before committing fully
  • ✅ Trust your gut — if something feels off, walk away — no obligation!
  • ✅ Report issues — help keep the community safe for everyone

💡 Business Pro Tips

  • ✅ Be specific — clear descriptions = better matches = faster deals
  • ✅ Value equally — skill swap = fair exchange. Be generous, be fair
  • ✅ Local first — in-person = faster trust, easier handover
  • ✅ Group buys = massive savings — team up with nearby businesses for supplies
  • ✅ Long-term wins — one swap often leads to ongoing work & referrals
  • ✅ Pay it forward — help someone starting out — your kindness returns!
