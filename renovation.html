🛡️ Safe & Responsible Practices

  • ✅ Gas & Electricity = Qualified Pros Only — never attempt yourself; always check credentials & certification
  • ✅ Meet in public first — for tool/material viewings; verify identity before work starts
  • ✅ Agree everything in writing — scope, price, timeline, materials, payment schedule
  • ✅ Check insurance — public liability, employer's liability, damage cover
  • ✅ Use proper PPE — goggles, mask, gloves, boots for dusty/noisy work
  • ✅ Waste disposal — recycle what you can; use licensed carrier for hazardous waste

💡 Renovation Pro Tips

  • ✅ Measure twice, cut once — old rule, still the best! Double-check everything
  • ✅ Buy 10% extra materials — cuts, mistakes, breakages happen; avoid colour-matching issues later
  • ✅ Strip first, plan second — you never know what's behind the walls until you look
  • ✅ Do the messy jobs first — plastering, painting, flooring = work from top down
  • ✅ Ask for help — friends, family, local community — many hands make light work!
  • ✅ Skill swap is powerful — trade what you can do for what you can't — it all balances out!
