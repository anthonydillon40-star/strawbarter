🛡️ Rehoming Rules — We Care About Animals

  • ✅ No selling for profit — adoption fee only to cover costs
  • ✅ All pets must be healthy, vaccinated & treated
  • ✅ No rehoming under 8 weeks old
  • ✅ Home check & meet-and-greet recommended
  • ✅ No breeding listings — pets only, not for sale
  • ✅ Report any suspicious listings — animal welfare first

🛒 Buying & Swapping Safely

  • ✅ Clean & wash all pre-loved items before use
  • ✅ Food must be sealed, in date & clearly labelled
  • ✅ Check for recalls — especially pet food & treats
  • ✅ Meet in public places for cash exchanges
  • ✅ Check items carefully before taking home
  • ✅ Ask questions — honest sellers welcome queries!
